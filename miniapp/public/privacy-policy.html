Welcome to PIN AI ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our AI assistant service.

PIN AI is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it.

Your information may be transferred to and processed on servers outside your country. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data is adequately protected during these transfers.
